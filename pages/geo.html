Straight Answers

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making a business visible — and accurately represented — inside AI-generated answers: the recommendations produced by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Claude. Where traditional SEO targets a page of blue links, GEO targets the single answer an AI gives when a customer asks for a recommendation. We use "AI visibility" and "GEO" interchangeably.

How is AI visibility different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in a list of results the customer still has to sift through. AI visibility determines whether you're named at all when an AI answers directly. The signals overlap — reviews, citations, structured data, consistent business information — but AI engines weight entity clarity, source authority, and factual consistency far more heavily than keywords. A business can rank well on Google and still be invisible to ChatGPT.

How long until I see results?

Meaningful score improvement typically takes 60–90 days after targeted work begins. AI engines refresh their understanding of local businesses more slowly than search indexes, so early fixes compound over the months that follow. That's also why the free audit matters: it establishes your baseline so progress is measurable, not vibes.
