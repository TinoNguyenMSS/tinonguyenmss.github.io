01 / Find your words

Start with the moment.

A shared detail. A playful question. A clear invitation. Pick the one that fits, then make it sound like you.

“I'm still defending my coffee order. Rematch this weekend?”

AFTER YOU MEET

If you actually joked about coffee, bring that moment back. Swap in a detail the two of you shared.

“You seem like trouble at trivia. Teammates or rivals?”

KEEP IT PLAYFUL

Use a shared interest to give her something fun to respond to. Keep the tone in step with your conversation.

“I'm enjoying this. Want to continue over coffee Saturday?”

ASK HER OUT

When the interest feels mutual, a simple invitation gives the conversation somewhere to go. Let her answer in her own time.
