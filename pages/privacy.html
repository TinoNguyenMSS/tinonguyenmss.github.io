Privacy Policy — MSS Core Scanner

Effective date: September 22, 2026

In brief: MSS Core Scanner is a website and AI-visibility auditing tool developed by Midnight Signal Studio. The scanner analyzes publicly accessible website information supplied for an audit. Optional AI-assisted reporting may send selected audit information to the AI provider chosen for that report.

1. Who We Are

MSS Core Scanner is operated by Midnight Signal Studio ("MSS", "we", "us", or "our").

2. Information Submitted for a Scan

When a scan is performed, the application may receive information entered by the user for the purpose of conducting the audit. This can include:

  • A website URL or other web address being audited.
  • Business name.
  • City or location information supplied for the audit.
  • Primary service information supplied for the audit.
  • A Google Business Profile URL supplied for the audit.
  • The user's selected AI reporting provider, if AI-assisted reporting is requested.

3. Public Website Information We Analyze

MSS Core Scanner retrieves publicly accessible information from the website being audited. Depending on the website, this may include the main webpage, robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap information, page titles and descriptions, headings, structured data such as JSON-LD, publicly linked social profiles, publicly displayed telephone-number information, and limited portions of publicly accessible page text.

The scanner also evaluates publicly available crawler directives, including whether specified AI crawler user agents are permitted or restricted by robots.txt.

4. How We Use Information

Information submitted to MSS Core Scanner is used to perform the requested audit and generate the resulting GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) analysis.

The scanner's core audit can operate using a deterministic, rules-based analysis engine without an external AI service.

5. Optional AI-Assisted Reporting

MSS Core Scanner may provide an optional AI-assisted executive summary. When an AI provider is selected, information derived from the completed audit may be transmitted to the selected AI service to generate the requested explanation.

Depending on the provider selected by the user, supported AI services may include Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic. The information sent for this purpose is audit-related information such as the audited business or URL, GEO score, findings, recommendations, and the scanner's underlying executive analysis.

The AI-assisted feature is separate from the scanner's core deterministic audit process. If AI-assisted reporting is not selected, the core audit does not require an external AI generation call.

6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

MSS Core Scanner does not require users to provide passwords, payment-card numbers, contact lists, photographs, precise device location, or other unrelated personal information in order to perform a website visibility audit.

7. Data Storage and Retention

The scanner server processes audit requests and returns the resulting audit to the requesting application. The current scanner server implementation does not contain a user-account database or a persistent database for storing completed scan results.

Information transmitted to an optional third-party AI provider may be subject to that provider's own privacy practices, retention policies, and terms. Users should review the applicable provider's privacy documentation when using AI-assisted reporting.

8. Third-Party Services

When optional AI functionality is used, MSS Core Scanner may communicate with third-party AI services in order to generate the requested report. These services process information according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

MSS does not control the privacy practices of third-party services and recommends reviewing their current policies before using optional third-party AI functionality.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the application to protect information handled by MSS Core Scanner. However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

MSS Core Scanner is a business-oriented technical auditing tool and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or MSS Core Scanner may be directed to Midnight Signal Studio through the official Midnight Signal Studio website.
